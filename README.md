# Financial Wealth Management

> **One clear, reliable view of your entire portfolio: assets, dividends and brokerage cash balances, all in one place.**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The average Brazilian retail investor holds accounts at more than one brokerage, invests across multiple asset classes (stocks, REITs/FIIs, fixed income, ETFs, BDRs, crypto) and still tracks everything in manual spreadsheets. The result: poor visibility, incorrect average cost calculations, unverified dividend payments and a painful annual tax filing.

This project is a platform that centralizes buy and sell transactions, updates market prices automatically, tracks dividends received and consolidates cash balances across all brokerages, giving investors a clear, real-time view of their net worth and returns.

## 2. The Problem

- **Fragmented portfolios:** positions spread across multiple brokerages, banks and crypto exchanges, with no consolidated view.
- **Manual, error-prone tracking:** spreadsheets require constant price updates, average cost calculations and manual handling of corporate actions (splits, reverse splits, stock bonuses).
- **Unverified dividends:** investors rarely know whether they received every dividend and interest on equity (JCP) payment they were entitled to, or how much passive income they earned in the year.
- **Forgotten cash:** idle money sitting in brokerage accounts with no visibility and no yield.
- **Complex income tax:** monthly capital gains calculations, exemptions and annual reports take hours and carry a high risk of error.

---

## 3. The Solution

A web platform built on four pillars:

### 3.1 Transaction Tracking
- Record buys and sells (date, asset, quantity, price, fees, brokerage).
- Automatic calculation of **average cost**, current position and **realized gains/losses**.
- Corporate action handling: splits, reverse splits, stock bonuses, rights offerings.
- Import brokerage trade confirmations (PDF) and spreadsheets (CSV/XLSX).

### 3.2 Live Market Prices
- Automatic price updates (stocks, REITs, ETFs, BDRs, crypto, indices).
- Portfolio market value and **returns** by asset, asset class and time period.
- Benchmark comparison (CDI, IBOV, IFIX, IPCA).

### 3.3 Dividends & Income
- Track dividends, interest on equity (JCP), REIT distributions and fixed-income interest.
- **Dividend calendar** (ex-date, payment date) with alerts for expected payments.
- Reconciliation: expected vs. received.
- Metrics: yield on cost, monthly and annual passive income.

### 3.4 Brokerage Cash Balances
- Track deposits, withdrawals and cash balance per brokerage.
- Reconciliation between recorded transactions and account statements.
- Consolidated view: **net worth = assets at market value + cash**.

---

## 4. Competitive Advantages

1. **Consolidated multi-brokerage view** that includes cash, not just assets.
2. **Dividend reconciliation** (expected vs. received), a feature largely overlooked by competitors.
3. **Maximum automation:** trade confirmation import, live prices and corporate actions with no manual work.
4. **Tax-focused** (roadmap): monthly capital gains, tax payment slips (DARF) and annual filing reports.
5. **Simple experience**, designed for everyday investors, not just power users.
